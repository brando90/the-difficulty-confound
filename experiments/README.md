# Experiment index

**Doc link:** <https://github.com/brando90/the-difficulty-confound/blob/main/experiments/README.md>

**TLDR:** Eleven controlled addition studies are complete and archived: exposure reverses difficulty rankings after exact answer matching and per-column carry-marginal matching (cutting input-digit TV distance by $>11\times$ to `0.0398`), constrained monotone ridge repairs extrapolation blow-up across held-out widths (up to 384), probed starting difficulty cuts held-out post-training loss RMSE by **47.20%** (Expt 05), **36.22%** (Expt 07), and **39.87%** (Expt 08 under RLVR)—plus **56.93%** on prequential online MDL (Expt 07)—when starting checkpoints have heterogeneous pretraining histories in MLPs, and cuts joint held-out prequential adaptation MDL RMSE by **18.84%** on causal autoregressive transformers under calibrated pretraining horizons (Expt 11). Control accuracy rises rather than falls after supervised balanced post-training (**+1.09 pp** in Expt 05, **+5.13 pp** in Expt 07, **+15.94 pp** in Expt 11) and pure 0/1 verifiable-reward RL (**+6.45 pp** in Expt 08). The full remediation gate passes in Expts 05 and 08 but not Expt 07 (pooled target accuracy 81.09%, below its 85% line) or Expt 11 (control CI lower bound 12.13 pp vs 15.0 pp line). A headroom-bounded RL utility law repairs the additive law's near-ceiling overshoot (**73.72%**, Expt 09). Replicating on autoregressive causal transformers shows that calibrating pretraining to $T_{\text{pre}}=300$ updates restores active headroom across all five widths (Expt 11), confirming pure compositional ranking reversal (**15.7 pp**) and starting-difficulty prequential MDL predictability (**18.84%**). All outcomes and reproduction artifacts are retained.

## Complexity-versus-alignment series (top-level `experiments/11_*` to `19_*`)

**Numbering note.** These folders were numbered in parallel with `archive/10_autoregressive_transformer_replication/` and `archive/11_transformer_calibrated_pretraining_horizon/` (PRs #13, #15), so two different studies carry the number 11. They are separate series and nothing overlaps on disk: the archive studies live under `archive/`, this series lives at the top level of `experiments/`. In this series' own files and commit messages, "Experiment 11" means `11_dfa_exact_complexity_vs_alignment/`, not `archive/11_transformer_calibrated_pretraining_horizon/`. The folders keep their names because runs in progress write to these paths.

These experiments cross exactly computable task complexity with hand-set data alignment; see the [complexity-versus-alignment overview](ideas/complexity_vs_alignment/README.md).
