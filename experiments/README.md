# Experiment index

**Doc link:** <https://github.com/brando90/the-difficulty-confound/blob/main/experiments/README.md>

**TLDR:** Eight controlled addition studies are complete and archived: exposure reverses difficulty rankings after exact answer matching and per-column carry-marginal matching (cutting input-digit TV distance by $>11\times$ to `0.0398`), constrained monotone ridge repairs extrapolation blow-up across held-out widths (up to 384), probed starting difficulty cuts held-out post-training loss RMSE by **47.20%** (Expt 05), **36.22%** (Expt 07), and **39.87%** (Expt 08 under RLVR)—plus **56.93%** on prequential online MDL (Expt 07)—when starting checkpoints have heterogeneous pretraining histories, and both supervised balanced post-training (**+1.09 pp** in Expt 05, **+5.13 pp** in Expt 07) and pure 0/1 verifiable-reward RL (**+6.45 pp** in Expt 08) pass the strict no-harm retention gate. All outcomes and reproduction artifacts are retained.
