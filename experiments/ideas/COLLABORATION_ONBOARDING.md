## 1. The Core Thesis in One Paragraph

**Is a benchmark task intrinsically hard, or has the model simply not practiced the right distribution yet?**
When we evaluate a model on a fixed skill (here, exact 3-digit integer addition with an unchanged ground-truth algorithm and unchanged test items), apparent difficulty rankings between "easy" (no-carry or single-carry) and "hard" (cascading two-carry) problems **reverse completely** depending on training exposure across final accuracy (in both feedforward MLPs and calibrated causal transformers) and—in the feedforward MLP studies—across **prequential (online) MDL code length** and **verifiable-reward RL improvement utility ($U = \text{Acc}_T - \text{Acc}_0$)**, even when we hold the exact answer histogram **and** the exact per-column carry frequencies ($50\%$ ones, $50\%$ tens) fixed across every training condition (on causal transformers in Expts 10–11, the online-MDL reversal gate is **NOT_ESTABLISHED**). Furthermore, when we want to **predict** held-out post-training performance across unseen model scales and unseen mixtures, an independent starting-difficulty probe ($D_{m_0}$) adds almost nothing ($1.94\%\text{–}3.48\%$) when all checkpoints share the same warmup history, but reduces held-out conditional loss RMSE by **47.20%** (Expt 05), **36.22%** (Expt 07), and **39.87%** (Expt 08 under RLVR)—plus **56.93%** on prequential online MDL in MLPs (Expt 07) and **18.84%** on prequential online MDL in calibrated causal transformers (Expt 11)—when checkpoints arrive with **heterogeneous prior training histories**. Finally, balanced post-training in `experiments/archive/05_pretraining_history_and_retention/` passes its strict no-harm retention gate (no-carry control **+1.09 [+0.33, +1.89] percentage points**, two-carry accuracy **90.70% [88.79, 92.43]**). In `experiments/archive/07_prequential_mdl_and_length_transfer/`, the single-carry control also improves (**+5.13 [+4.13, +6.38] percentage points**) and gap recovery is **104.29% [101.45, 107.53]**, but Gate 4 is **NOT_ESTABLISHED**: pooled cascading-two-carry accuracy is **81.09% [79.51, 82.88]**, below the prespecified 85% line (width 32 is **27.80%** after remediation; width 64 is **82.26%**). In `experiments/archive/08_rlvr_verifiable_reward_utility_laws/`, the verifiable-reward remediation gate is **SUPPORTED** (control **+6.45 [+4.69, +7.91] percentage points**). Its pooled additive utility law is **NOT_ESTABLISHED** (**-15.78% [-35.91, 3.15]**), which `experiments/archive/09_headroom_bounded_rlvr_utility_law/` repairs by bounding utility by remaining headroom (**73.72% [70.43, 77.26]**). On causal transformers, Gate 4 is **NOT_ESTABLISHED** in both `experiments/archive/10_autoregressive_transformer_replication/` (pooled initial gap **2.84 [2.01, 3.86] pp** under ceiling saturation vs 15.0 pp minimum) and `experiments/archive/11_transformer_calibrated_pretraining_horizon/` (initial-gap 95% lower bound **12.13 pp** vs 15.0 pp minimum, though recovery **201.55%**, target accuracy **99.90%**, and control change **+15.94 [+14.18, +17.80] pp** pass their lines).

## 3. Repository Workflow & Scientific Hygiene Rules

Every experiment follows a strict 5-step workflow so that neither human nor agent can tune post-hoc thresholds:

1. **Freeze `PROTOCOL.md` before running:** State the exact research question, fit/held-out splits, mathematical functional forms, and numerical pass/fail gates (`SUPPORTED` vs `NOT_ESTABLISHED`) *before* training models.
2. **Preflight `--check-only` & unit tests:**
   - `python3 experiments/archive/<id>/expt_v1/test_laws.py` verifies law constraints and independent cross-fitter parity.
   - `python3 experiments/archive/<id>/expt_v1/run.py --check-only` verifies exact dataset cardinalities, train/probe/eval disjointness, and marginal invariants without training.
3. **Run & Replay-Verify (`verify.py`):**
   - Training saves every checkpoint's `.pt` weights and per-item predictions.
   - `python3 experiments/archive/<id>/expt_v1/verify.py` reloads every saved checkpoint, recomputes forward passes independently, checks greedy accuracy against saved tables, and requires bit-loss differences `<= 1e-6`. Completed studies are under `experiments/archive/`, not `experiments/<id>/`. For Experiments 05–11 the verifier falls back to the sibling copy of `experiments/archive/02_heldout_exposure_difficulty_laws/` after the archive move, and that path fallback is the only source file allowed to differ from the training hash.
4. **Seed-Block Bootstrap Uncertainty (`analyze.py` & `report.py`):**
   - Never treat individual test items or checkpoints from the same seed as independent! All 95% confidence intervals use paired bootstrap resampling over the **8 independent seed blocks** (`20,000` replicates, the `bootstrap_draws` value in each experiment config).
5. **Retain All Counterevidence:**
   - When a gate is `NOT_ESTABLISHED`, report it with the same prominence as `SUPPORTED` gates. Never drop outlier seeds or failed forecasts.

Key open avenues for subsequent investigation:

1. **Reverse vs Forward Generation Order (Inductive Bias Study):**
   - In standard digit addition, computing sum digits from LSB to MSB matches the directional flow of carries (right-to-left), whereas MSB-first requires multi-step lookahead. Does generation order interact with pretraining exposure, compositional carry transfer, and sample-efficiency in causal transformers?
2. **RLVR on Autoregressive Transformers with Headroom-Bounded Laws:**
   - Combining the headroom-bounded RLVR utility law from Experiment 09 with causal autoregressive generation on arithmetic chains.
3. **Length Extrapolation (e.g., 5-Digit Addition):**
   - Testing whether carry-chain transfer extends to longer token contexts where multi-hop carry cascades span 4–5 steps.
